Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how PlanProof handles information shared through this website. It is a general policy and is not a substitute for legal advice. If you have specific compliance questions, we recommend speaking with a qualified lawyer.

Who we are

PlanProof designs and facilitates business continuity tabletop exercises for organizations in the UAE and wider GCC. This policy covers the website at plan-proof.com and the request forms on it. It does not cover third-party sites we link to.

What we collect

Depending on which form you use, we may collect:

  • Contact details you provide, such as your name, mobile number, and email address
  • Information about your organization you choose to share, such as industry, size, location, and risk concerns, if you use the generic scenario intake
  • Business continuity plan documents or details you choose to share with us, if you use the plan-review option
  • Any other information you include in a message to us, such as remarks or pricing inquiries
  • Walkthrough requests: your name, company, email address and mobile number, if you ask for a walkthrough of the full demo

We do not currently use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts on this site. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

How your information reaches us

When you submit a form on this website, your submission, including any plan document you attach, is received and stored by our website host's form service (Netlify Forms). A copy of the submission is then sent to our business inbox by an email delivery service (Resend). Your contact details and the answers you type into the form are also saved to a private database (Supabase) so we can keep track of inquiries and respond to them; any plan document you attach is not copied into this database. Submissions are held in private accounts accessible only to us; they are never published or made publicly accessible.

If you prefer not to use the forms, you can email us directly instead.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and design or deliver a tabletop exercise
  • To provide pricing information you've requested
  • To communicate with you about a service you've asked about

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent personal data to third parties.

Confidentiality of shared plans

Business continuity plans or organizational details you share with us are reviewed directly, one-to-one, and are never published or made publicly accessible. Materials are deleted after your exercise package is delivered unless you ask us to retain them; only anonymised insights that cannot identify you are kept, as described under “How long we keep it” below. A mutual non-disclosure agreement is available on request before you share sensitive material.

Who else sees it

We use a small number of service providers to run this business, who may process information on our behalf:

  • Website hosting and form processing (Netlify) — to serve this website and to receive and store form submissions and attached files; hosting providers may also log standard technical data such as IP addresses as part of normal web traffic
  • Email delivery (Resend) — to send a copy of each form submission to our inbox
  • Database hosting (Supabase) — to keep a private record of your contact details and form answers so we can track and respond to your inquiry; attached plan documents are not stored there
  • Email hosting (Zoho Mail) — to receive and store messages sent to our inbox
  • Web fonts (Google Fonts) — the fonts on this site are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address when the page loads

Some of these providers may process or store data on servers located outside the UAE. Each provider applies its own security measures to the data it handles for us.

We do not otherwise share your information with third parties except where required by law.

How long we keep it

Contact and inquiry information is kept for as long as needed to respond to you and, if you become a client, to deliver and support your engagement.

After your exercise package is delivered, we delete the materials you shared. We may retain anonymised, aggregated insights (for example, common gaps by sector) that cannot identify your organization or any individual, to improve future exercises.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, including the UAE's Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Withdraw consent for us to hold your information, where consent is the basis for processing

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below.

Governing law

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent version.
