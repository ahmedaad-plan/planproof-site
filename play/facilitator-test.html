    Send everyone back to the waiting screen?
    Every device returns to the waiting screen and the exercise starts again from the first inject when you press Start. Notes and messages already written are kept and labelled "Earlier run". This is for testing and for starting over — not for a short pause.
    Setup — rooms, links and QR codes, departments
    How many rooms are running this exercise? Room 1 is always your own (the facilitator's) room, so "3" means your room plus 2 more. You can change this as many times as you like before clicking "Start exercise" — it locks once the exercise is running.

    Departments

    Add any department missing from the list — at any time, also during the exercise. Only you can add one, so nobody can invent a department. A department added during the exercise shows "Not required" for the injects it missed (including the one running when you add it), and is counted like every other department from the next inject on.

    Expected attendance (optional)

    Fill in what you expect — a week ahead or on the day — and the list on the right shows who is missing or extra. Advice only: Start is never blocked. You can change the numbers at any time.
