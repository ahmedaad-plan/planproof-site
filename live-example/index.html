A PlanProof exercise is not read from paper. Each message is played live, in the room and on every phone, so your teams respond under the same pressure as in a real incident.

A news clip from Marsa News, the fictional channel used in our exercises, as it plays on the room screen.

  • The room screen shows each message with a large countdown.
  • A natural voice reads it aloud; news arrives as short clips from a fictional channel.
  • Every participant answers on their own phone.
  • The facilitator controls one shared clock for every room.
